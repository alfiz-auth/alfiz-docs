Alfiz ships as two packages: `@alfiz/core` holds the evaluator — the catalog, grammar, closures, and check logic — and `@alfiz/application` wraps a storage driver into the full local provider. You define your permissions in TypeScript, wire up storage, and every `can()` check runs in-process against your own database with no external round-trips required.
